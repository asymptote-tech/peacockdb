Paths below are relative to `/media/data/peacockdb`. Modes: the five are tp1-single, tp1-rowgroup,
tp4-single, tp4-rowgroup, tp4-sized. "cpu" = `test_cpu_corpus`, "gpu" = `test_gpu_corpus`. A gpu
cell is only ever enabled where the cpu cell is, so a cpu disablement also removes the gpu cell.

- **#177, #179, #142, #145, #154, #155, #170** — pricing / handle-copy / accounting. #145 is the *mechanism* #152 wants but disables nothing by itself.
